## Purchasing a Domain with Namecheap

### Overview

With a phishing domain selected, this lesson walks through the process of purchasing a domain using a registrar—in this case, **Namecheap**. Registering a realistic, low-cost domain is foundational for deploying phishing landing pages and configuring services such as email (via G Suite), SSL, and DNS later in the engagement.

This is a practical, step-by-step guide to securing and managing a phishing domain.

---

### Key Concepts

- **Domain Registrars** There are many domain registrars available. **Namecheap** is recommended for its affordability, ease of use, and quick access to DNS settings, which will be critical later in the phishing setup.
- **Domain Search and Selection**
    - Enter the domain name you've chosen (e.g., `hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam`) to check availability.
    - Choose a domain that is cheap (typically $1–$3/year) and convincing enough for your phishing engagement.
- **Purchasing Process on Namecheap**  
    The tutorial walks through the entire purchase workflow:

1. 1. Create a Namecheap account
    2. Search and select a domain
    3. Add the domain to cart and proceed to checkout
    4. Disable auto-renew (since the domain is disposable for testing)
    5. Enable domain privacy protection to prevent personal information from appearing in WHOIS records
    6. Complete billing and payment (credit card or PayPal)

- **Post-Purchase Configuration**  
    Once the domain is registered, students are shown how to access it in their Namecheap dashboard. This step confirms ownership and prepares the environment for:
    - DNS configuration
    - SSL certificate setup
    - Google Workspace (G Suite) integration
    - Landing page hosting

---

### Practical Considerations

- **Domain Privacy Is Essential**  
    Always enable domain privacy to protect your personal information, especially during red team or phishing simulations.
- **Turn Off Auto-Renewal**  
    As this domain is likely for testing or educational use, students should disable auto-renew to avoid unnecessary charges.
- **Affordability**  
    Domains used for phishing exercises should be low-cost and easily disposable. `.cam` is cited as an effective, inexpensive option that resembles `.com` visually.
