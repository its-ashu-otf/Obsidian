## Billing Notice – Avoid Unintended Charges

As part of this course, you're working with cloud infrastructure on **DigitalOcean**, which offers a powerful but usage-sensitive platform.

### Always-On Billing for Droplets (VMs)

Please be aware that **DigitalOcean charges for droplets (virtual machines) whether they are actively running or not**. Simply powering off a VM does **not** stop billing. To avoid unnecessary charges:

- **Delete droplets** you no longer need.
- Or **create a snapshot** if you plan to pause progress (more on that below).

### $200 Free Credit (If You Used the Link)

If you signed up using the link provided in **Section 2.1 – Creating an Account on DigitalOcean**, you should have received **$200 in free credits**. This credit will be applied automatically to your account.

### Use It Within 60 Days

The $200 credit is valid for **60 days from the time of account creation**. We **strongly recommend** completing the course within that time frame to avoid incurring real charges. This credit will cover everything you need for the course (and much more).

If you anticipate needing more time:

1. **Create a snapshot** of your VM (droplet) to preserve its state.
2. **Delete the droplet** to stop billing.
3. You can return later, re-deploy from the snapshot, and resume your work.
