# Lab Guide: Creating Your DigitalOcean Account

In this lab, you’ll create your **own cloud server environment** using [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/) — a popular platform for lightweight, low-cost cloud hosting. This server will serve as the core infrastructure for your phishing campaigns and lab exercises.

---

## Why DigitalOcean?

While AWS, Azure, and GCP are popular cloud providers, **DigitalOcean** is the preferred choice for this course because:

- ✅ **Simplified pricing** — easier to understand than AWS/GCP.
- ✅ **Beginner-friendly interface** — minimal setup friction.
- ✅ **Less restrictive** — tends to allow more flexibility for lab-style infrastructure, including phishing simulations.

> ⚠️ You _can_ use AWS or Azure if you’re comfortable — but this guide (and the course) will focus specifically on DigitalOcean.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step: Create Your Account

### 1. **Navigate to DigitalOcean**

Go to: [https://www.digitalocean.com](https://www.digitalocean.com/)

Click **"Sign up with Email"**.

---

### 2. **Enter Your Information**

Fill in the following fields:

- **Full Name**:
- **Email**: Use a working email address (e.g., `[your.name@example.com](mailto:your.name@example.com)`)
- **Password**: Use a secure, unique password. Preferably managed via a password manager.

Click **Sign Up**.

---

### 3. **Confirm You’re Human**

You may be prompted to complete a CAPTCHA.

---

### 4. **Confirm Your Email**

Open your inbox and look for a message from DigitalOcean.

Click the **confirmation link** inside the email.

---

### 5. **Initial Account Setup**

Once confirmed, you’ll be prompted to answer a few onboarding questions:

- **What best describes your role?**
    - Select: `Student`
- **What are you planning to build?**
    - Select: `Website or Content Site`  
        (✔️ This fits our phishing landing page use case)
- **How many teammates will collaborate with you?**
    - Select: `Just me`

Click **Submit** to finish onboarding.

---

### 6. **Access the Control Panel**

After submitting, you’ll be taken to the **DigitalOcean dashboard**. This is where you’ll:

- Launch Droplets (servers)
- Manage projects
- Monitor billing
- Access terminal (via browser or SSH)

No further action is needed for this lab — you’ve now created your cloud environment!
