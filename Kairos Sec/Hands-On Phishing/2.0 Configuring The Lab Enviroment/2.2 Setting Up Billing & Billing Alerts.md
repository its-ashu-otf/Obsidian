# 💳 Lab Guide: Setting Up Billing & Billing Alerts in DigitalOcean

Before you can deploy a server (called a _Droplet_ in DigitalOcean), you must complete your **billing setup**. Even though the course falls within **free or near-free usage**, setting a **billing alert** is critical to avoid surprise charges — especially if you're experimenting beyond the core labs.

---

## Why Billing Alerts Matter

Cloud horror stories are real — students and pros alike have accidentally left resources running, racking up hundreds (or thousands) of dollars. This simple step prevents that risk:

> “If you do just one thing to protect your wallet — set up billing alerts.”

---

## Step-by-Step: Set Up Billing

### 1. **Access the Billing Section**

From your DigitalOcean Dashboard:

- Look for **"Billing (action needed)"** on the left-hand menu.
- Click it to open the billing screen.

---

### 2. **Add a Payment Method**

Click **"Add payment method"** in the upper-right corner.

You can use:

- 💳 **Credit/Debit Card**
- 🅿️ **PayPal**
- 🇨🇳 **Alipay** (for supported regions)

Fill in:

- Full name
- Country
- Billing address
- Card details or linked PayPal info

Click **"Add Payment Method"** once your info is filled out.

---

## Step-by-Step: Set Up Billing Alerts

### 1. **Go to Billing Settings**

- In the same billing page, click the **“Settings”** tab.

### 2. **Scroll to Billing Alerts**

- Locate the **Billing Alert** section (scroll down).
- Click **"Enable Alert"** (on the right side).

### 3. **Set Your Alert Threshold**

- Enter an alert amount: `5.00` USD is recommended for this course.

This means:

- You’ll receive an email if charges on your account exceed $5.

Click **"Confirm"** to activate the alert.
