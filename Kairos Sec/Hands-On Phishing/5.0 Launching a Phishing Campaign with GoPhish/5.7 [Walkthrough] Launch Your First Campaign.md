

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-objective)**Objective**

In this culminating lab, you will officially launch your **first phishing campaign** using the full infrastructure you've configured throughout the course—including email templates, landing pages, sending profiles, and user targets—all hosted on a **DigitalOcean droplet** using **GoPhish**.

The goal is to simulate a complete phishing workflow from **delivery to credential capture**, while understanding real-world challenges like spam filtering, firewall rules, and campaign analytics.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-lab-setup-checklist)**Lab Setup Checklist**

Before launching, confirm the following are ready:

- Landing page hosted over HTTPS (port 443 enabled)
- Email template(s) tested and spam-tuned
- Sending profile successfully tested via Gmail SMTP
- Reverse SSH tunnel is active (to bypass port blocks)
- Target users imported into GoPhish
- DigitalOcean **firewall rules configured**

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-firewall-configuration)**Firewall Configuration**

Victims will not be able to access your phishing landing page unless the proper firewall rules are set in DigitalOcean.

> **Required Rule Additions**:

- **HTTP (port 80)**: Allow from `0.0.0.0/0`
- **HTTPS (port 443)**: Allow from `0.0.0.0/0`

These ensure that any recipient—regardless of their IP—can open your phishing link without being blocked.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-steps-to-launch-the-campaign)**Steps to Launch the Campaign**

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-1-log-into-gophish-admin)**1. Log into GoPhish Admin**

- Ensure you're accessing GoPhish via your **domain name** (not just the IP) to reflect real-world conditions.

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-2-create-a-new-campaign)**2. Create a New Campaign**

- Navigate to **Campaigns > New Campaign**.
- Fill out the fields:
    - **Name**: Give it a descriptive name (e.g., _First Phishing Trip_)
    - **Email Template**: Choose the template you've tested (e.g., _WordPress Login_, _Less Scammy_)
    - **Landing Page**: Select the spoofed login page you created
    - **URL**: Your phishing domain (e.g., `[https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam](https://hacksmartermanufacturing.cam/)`)
    - **Sending Profile**: Choose the working Gmail-based SMTP profile
    - **User Group(s)**: Select the group(s) you created earlier

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-3-send-a-test-email-first)**3. Send a Test Email First**

- Before launching the full campaign, send a **test email to yourself** to confirm deliverability and formatting.
- Check your inbox **and spam/junk folder**.
- If it’s flagged as spam, revise your subject line, greeting, or remove suspicious keywords (like “credentials” or “urgent”).

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-4-launch-the-campaign)**4. Launch the Campaign**

- Once satisfied, return to the campaign screen and **click “Launch Campaign”**.
- Your emails will be queued and sent according to the settings you've configured.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-monitoring-the-campaign)**Monitoring the Campaign**

In the campaign dashboard, GoPhish tracks in real time:

- **Emails Sent**
- **Emails Opened** (via tracking pixel)
- **Links Clicked**
- **Credentials Submitted**

This lets you analyze engagement metrics just like in a real red team or phishing simulation.

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/58-walkthrough-launch-your-first-campaign#user-content-troubleshooting-tips)**Troubleshooting Tips**

- **Emails not sending?** Rerun your reverse SSH command:

ssh -N -R 2525

.gmail.com:587 root@[your-droplet-ip]

- **Landing page not accessible?** Re-check firewall rules for ports 80 and 443.
- **Spam filtering?** Modify the email to use plain text, tone down the subject, and avoid spammy keywords.
