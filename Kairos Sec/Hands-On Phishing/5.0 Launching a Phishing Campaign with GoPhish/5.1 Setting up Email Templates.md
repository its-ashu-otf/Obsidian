### **Objectives**

- Navigate and understand the GoPhish administrative interface.
- Create a custom phishing **email template** using dynamic variables.
- Connect your domain, landing page, and sending profile to simulate a credible attack.
- Build and prepare for your first **user-targeted phishing campaign**.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/51-setting-up-email-templates#user-content-gophish-dashboard-components-overview)**GoPhish Dashboard Components Overview**

- **Campaigns**: Where you launch and track phishing simulations.
- **Users and Groups**: Allows importing of target users (manually or via CSV).
- **Email Templates**: Core focus of this video; where phishing emails are created.
- **Landing Pages**: Hosts the cloned site or credential capture form (e.g., fake WordPress login).
- **Sending Profiles**: Connects your domain to Google Workspace for email delivery.
- **Tracking & Analytics**: Tracks opens, clicks, and submissions via embedded tracking pixels.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/51-setting-up-email-templates#user-content-creating-a-phishing-email-template)**Creating a Phishing Email Template**

**Basic Setup**:

- Click **“New Template”** and name it (e.g., _WordPress Login Template_).
- Set the **Envelope Sender** (e.g., `[admin@hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam](mailto:admin@hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam)`).
- Enter an **email subject line** and compose your body.

**Personalization with Variables**:

- Use GoPhish dynamic variables such as:
    - - `{{.FirstName}}` – Personalizes greetings.
            - `{{.Email}}` – Embeds the recipient’s email for legitimacy.
    - `{{.URL}}` – Inserts the campaign’s malicious link.
- Example:

Hello {{.FirstName}}, Please log into the portal using the following link: {{.URL}}

**Embedding a Tracking Image**:

- Add a **tracking image** to detect when emails are opened.
- Useful for confirming inbox delivery and gauging campaign effectiveness.

**Optional Enhancements**:

- Use **HTML formatting** to make the email appear professional.
    
- Add branding (logos, footers, styles) from the real company to enhance realism.
