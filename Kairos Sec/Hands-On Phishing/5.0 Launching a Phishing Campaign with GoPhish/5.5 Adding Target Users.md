### **Objective**

In this lab, you will learn how to **add users to your phishing campaign** within the GoPhish platform, either manually or via bulk import. This step is essential for executing a realistic and structured phishing engagement, especially when targeting multiple users or departments across an organization.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-scenario-context)**Scenario Context**

You’ve crafted a phishing email and built a spoofed landing page. Now it's time to define who your targets are. In a real-world red team or social engineering engagement, this step would align with the **reconnaissance phase**—where you identify key personnel, their roles, and email addresses.

For this lab, you'll simulate that process by creating one or more **user groups** in GoPhish using test or disposable email addresses.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-lab-tasks)**Lab Tasks**

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-1-navigate-to-users-and-groups)**1. Navigate to Users and Groups**

- From the GoPhish dashboard, click on **“Users & Groups”** in the sidebar.
- Click **“New Group”** to begin creating your target list.

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-2-choose-an-import-method)**2. Choose an Import Method**

- **Manual Entry**: Add users one at a time—ideal for **spear phishing** or small test groups.
- **Bulk Import**: Recommended for **larger campaigns**.
    - Download the provided **CSV template** from GoPhish.
- Fill in the required fields:
    - - First Name
            - Last Name
            - Email Address
    - Position
    - Use a CSV editor (e.g., Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice) to populate the file.
    - Upload your completed CSV to bulk import users into the group.

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-3-create-multiple-groups-optional-but-recommended)**3. Create Multiple Groups (Optional but Recommended)**

- Organize your users into distinct groups (e.g., by department or level of privilege).
- This allows for:
    - **Staggered email sends**
    - **A/B testing** of different email templates
    - Tracking how different roles respond to phishing attempts

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-4-use-realistic-but-controlled-data)**4. Use Realistic but Controlled Data**

- Use **your own email address** or multiple variations of it for testing.
- Do **not** attempt to use the demo emails from Hack Smarter Manufacturing—they are placeholders and will not work.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/55-adding-target-users#user-content-key-takeaways)**Key Takeaways**

- Organizing users into groups enables **targeted delivery**, better **tracking**, and more **realistic testing scenarios**.
- Using the bulk import function saves time and supports larger campaign builds.
- Effective user grouping allows you to adapt campaigns based on **user behavior** and **response trends**.
