### **Objective**

Your task in this lab is to **design a phishing email that is both convincing and effective**, targeting users at the fictional organization _HackSmarter Manufacturing_. This exercise emphasizes realism, attention to detail, and strategic thinking—key elements in any successful phishing campaign.

### **Scenario Context**

You’ve gathered key reconnaissance:

- _HackSmarter Manufacturing_ is using **WordPress** for its internal operations.
- You have a functional **malicious landing page** that mimics the WordPress login interface.
- You’ve enumerated valid users at the organization and are now ready to execute your first phishing campaign.

This lab will simulate the **initial phishing lure**, which aims to entice the user to click your link and enter credentials on the spoofed site.

---

### **Requirements**

Using **GoPhish**, complete the following steps:

**Create a New Email Template**:

- Name the template appropriately (e.g., _WordPress Login Alert_).
- Use a relevant and urgent **subject line** that aligns with your narrative.

**Configure the Sender Address**:

- Use your registered domain (e.g., `[admin@hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam](mailto:admin@hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam)`) as the envelope sender.

**Write the Email Body**:

- Avoid generic greetings like “Dear user.” Instead, use **GoPhish variables** such as:
    - `{{.FirstName}}` for personalized greetings.
    - `{{.Email}}` to reinforce legitimacy.
    - `{{.URL}}` to embed your landing page link.

**Polish the Design**:

- Enhance the appearance using basic **HTML** formatting.
- Optionally include company branding or formatting based on HackSmarter’s style (logos, colors, layout).

---

### **Challenge Guidelines**

- Your phishing email **should not look like a phishing email**. Make it as realistic and persuasive as possible.
- Maintain a **professional tone** and logical premise (e.g., a password reset alert or security verification notice).
- **Creativity is encouraged**—leverage what you know about phishing, social engineering, and email behavior.
