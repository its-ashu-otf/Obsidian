### **Objective**

In this lab, you will configure a **malicious landing page** that mirrors the login interface of a real WordPress site. This page will serve as the destination for your phishing email, simulating a realistic environment where victims may unknowingly enter their credentials.

This is a critical piece in the phishing workflow: without a convincing landing page, even the most well-crafted phishing email may fail.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/54-configuring-the-landing-page#user-content-context)**Context**

Your target—**HackSmarter Manufacturing**—uses a self-hosted WordPress login portal. You've already identified the real login page and crafted an email to lure victims. Now it’s time to clone that page and deploy it through **GoPhish**.

The objective is to make the page look as close as possible to the legitimate one while **optionally capturing submitted credentials** and redirecting users to avoid suspicion.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/54-configuring-the-landing-page#user-content-tasks-and-configuration-steps)**Tasks and Configuration Steps**

**Clone the Real WordPress Login Page**:

- In GoPhish, go to **Landing Pages** and click **“New Page”**.
- Name it something like _WordPress Login Landing Page_.
- Use the **“Import Site”** feature to pull in the real login page from Hack Smarter Manufacturing.
- This will auto-clone the HTML and design, making it look visually authentic.

**Enable Data Capture Options**:

- Select **“Capture Submitted Data”** to log usernames.
- Optionally, enable **“Capture Passwords”** (check with client policy—this may not be permitted in all engagements).
- Understand that captured data is stored in **clear text** and should be handled with caution.

**Add a Redirect URL**:

- After form submission, users should be redirected to the **real** login page.
- This minimizes suspicion and mimics a genuine login error or session timeout.
- Use the real Hack Smarter Manufacturing login URL.

**Save and Preview the Page**:

- After saving, use the **Preview** function to confirm that the cloned login page appears identical to the original.
- Validate that the URL displays your spoofed domain (e.g., `hacksmarter-manufacturing.cam`) but the content matches the target.

By the end of this lab, you will have a **functioning phishing site** ready for deployment in your campaign. This page, combined with your previously built email template, forms the core of your social engineering engagement.
