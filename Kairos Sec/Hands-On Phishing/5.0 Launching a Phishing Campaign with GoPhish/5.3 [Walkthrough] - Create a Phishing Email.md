### **Objective**

This lab demonstrates how to craft a **polished, believable phishing email** using a realistic business pretext, free tooling (such as ChatGPT), and basic HTML. You'll observe how to integrate dynamic variables and refine messaging to increase deliverability and decrease detection by spam filters.

---

### **Approach Demonstrated**

**Identifying the Pretext**:

- The phishing email is based on a system update to HackSmarter’s **monitoring dashboards**, a feature mentioned on their homepage.
- The email prompts users to log in and verify access, lending authenticity without appearing urgent or suspicious.

**Using ChatGPT for Email Generation**:

- The free version of ChatGPT is used to draft a **clean HTML email template**.
- Dynamic placeholders are included for the **landing page URL** and **personalized greeting**.

**Customizing the HTML Email**:

- Added GoPhish variables:
    - `{{.FirstName}}` for personalized greetings.
    - `{{.URL}}` for the malicious link to the spoofed WordPress login.
    - Avoided common spam triggers like “action required” or “credentials” in the subject and body.
- Adjusted wording to sound professional but low-friction:
    - Example: “access the dashboard with your WordPress information.”

**Final Touches**:

- The subject line is simplified to “Dashboard Update.”
- The message is previewed and validated in GoPhish.
- The completed email template is saved for use in the upcoming phishing campaign.

---

### **Key Takeaways**

- **Credibility and subtlety** are crucial in phishing email design.
- **Using AI tools and reconnaissance** can dramatically improve the quality of your phishing content.
- **Language and tone** matter—avoid urgent, threatening, or keyword-heavy content that may flag spam filters.
- Personalization using GoPhish variables helps the email appear more authentic.
