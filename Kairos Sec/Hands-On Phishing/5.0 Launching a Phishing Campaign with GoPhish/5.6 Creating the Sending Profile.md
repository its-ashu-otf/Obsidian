### **Objective**

In this lab, you’ll configure a **sending profile in GoPhish** to deliver phishing emails using a **Google Workspace account** tied to your malicious domain. You’ll also implement a **reverse SSH tunnel** to bypass **SMTP port restrictions** enforced by cloud providers like DigitalOcean.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-scenario-context)**Scenario Context**

Many cloud providers block direct outbound SMTP traffic to prevent abuse. To work around this, you will:

- Use a trusted provider (**Google Workspace**) for sending emails.
- Set up a **reverse SSH tunnel** from your **Kali Linux VM** to the **DigitalOcean Droplet** to route outbound email.
- Create and configure the appropriate **SMTP relay and app passwords**.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-step-by-step-instructions)**Step-by-Step Instructions**

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-1-google-workspace-setup-httpssupportgooglecomaccountsanswer185833hlen)**1. Google Workspace Setup (**[https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833?hl=en](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833?hl=en))

- Enable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** for the Google account.
- Generate an **App Password** for GoPhish use.
- Set up the **SMTP Relay** in Google Admin:
    - Allow any sender address.
    - Require SMTP authentication.
    - Restrict to your **Kali VM’s public IP** (`curl ifconfig.me`).

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-2-create-the-reverse-ssh-tunnel)**2. Create the Reverse SSH Tunnel**

Use this command **from your Kali VM** to tunnel SMTP traffic through Google:

ssh -N -R 2525

.gmail.com:587 root@[digital-ocean-ip]

> **Explanation**:

- `-N`: No remote command will be executed.
- `-R 2525:smtp-relay.gmail.com:587`: Forwards port `2525` on the **DigitalOcean Droplet** to Google’s SMTP relay via your **local machine**.
- `root@[digital-ocean-ip]`: Replace this with the **public IP address of your DigitalOcean Droplet**.

Once this tunnel is active, any connection made to `127.0.0.1:2525` on the droplet will be routed through your Kali box and sent out via Google SMTP—**bypassing any port restrictions**.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-3-configure-sending-profile-in-gophish)**3. Configure Sending Profile in GoPhish**

- Navigate to **Sending Profiles > New Profile**.
- Use the following values:
    - **Host**: `127.0.0.1`
    - **Port**: `2525`
    - **Username**: Your full Google Workspace email address.
    - **Password**: The **App Password** you generated (with no spaces).

Test the configuration by sending a test email. If the tunnel is active and the SMTP relay is working, your test message should arrive successfully.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/56-creating-the-sending-profile#user-content-4-enable-https-in-gophish)**4. Enable HTTPS in GoPhish**

To make the phishing landing page more convincing:

- Open your GoPhish `config.json` and update:

"listen_url": "0.0.0.0:443", "use_tls": true

- Restart GoPhish to serve your landing page over HTTPS.
