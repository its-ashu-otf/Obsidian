### What is a Phishlet?

A **phishlet** is a YAML-based configuration that tells Evilginx2 how to:

- Proxy a target website
- Capture login credentials
- Steal session cookies and authentication tokens
- Redirect traffic to a legitimate service invisibly

Phishlets form the foundation of every Evilginx2 phishing scenario. Each one is tailored to a specific application (e.g., Google, WordPress, Amazon) and includes logic for domain handling, session hijacking, and credential harvesting.

---

### Why It Matters

Phishlets allow attackers (or red teamers in a legal context) to **bypass 2FA** by stealing the session cookie after the victim successfully logs into the real service — unaware that anything malicious is occurring. This is a key differentiator between Evilginx and simpler phishing tools.
