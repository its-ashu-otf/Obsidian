# Re-using Stolen Session Cookies

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-objective)Objective

In this lab, you’ll **manually reuse a stolen session token** captured through Evilginx to **bypass the login process** and access a WordPress dashboard without needing the username, password, or even 2FA.

This is the most impactful part of Evilginx’s capabilities — and a critical skill in red team engagements focused on **MFA bypass and post-authentication access**.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-why-this-matters)Why This Matters

Most phishing tools stop at capturing credentials. Evilginx goes further by allowing you to:

- Capture **active session tokens**
- Reuse them to bypass even **multi-factor authentication (MFA)**
- Gain **immediate access to the authenticated area** of a target app (e.g., `/wp-admin`)

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-prerequisites)Prerequisites

- You’ve already captured a successful session via Evilginx
- You have:
    - The **wordpress_logged_in** and **wordpress_sec** cookies (from `sessions`)
    - Access to a browser (Firefox recommended)
- The target is:

> `<https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop/wp-admin>`

> **Ethical Reminder:** You are only authorized to access the shared WordPress lab environment as part of this course. **Do not change passwords or content**.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-step-by-step-instructions)Step-by-Step Instructions

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-1-view-captured-session-in-evilginx)1. **View Captured Session in Evilginx**

In your Evilginx terminal:

```
sessions
```

Identify the correct session ID (e.g., `2`), then:

`sessions 2`

Locate the **cookie for the path** `**/wp-admin**`. You're looking for a name like:

- `wordpress_sec_xxxxxxxxx`  
    And a long token as its value.

Also take note of the cookie for:

- `/` — typically `wordpress_logged_in_xxxxxx`

---

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-2-open-the-real-wordpress-login-in-firefox-private-window)2. **Open the Real WordPress Login in Firefox (Private Window)**

Navigate to:

[https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop/wp-admin](https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop/wp-admin)

You'll be redirected to the login page.

---

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-3-inject-the-stolen-cookies-using-dev-tools)3. **Inject the Stolen Cookies Using Dev Tools**

1. Press `F12` to open **Web Developer Tools**
2. Click the **Storage** tab
3. Under **Cookies**, select `hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop`
4. Delete any existing cookies
5. Add the stolen session cookie

---

#### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-4-access-the-admin-area)4. **Access the Admin Area**

After injecting the cookies, navigate to:

[https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop/wp-admin](https://hacksmarter-manufacturing.shop/wp-admin)

You should now be logged in as **Tony**, without using the login form.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/69-walkthrough-re-using-stolen-session-cookies#user-content-what-this-demonstrates)What This Demonstrates

You’ve now demonstrated the core red team technique that Evilginx2 enables:

> **Full session hijacking, even on MFA-protected accounts.**

The only limitation is how long the session remains valid before expiration or invalidation.
