# Destroy Your Digital Ocean Droplet

Now that you’ve completed the hands-on portion involving **Evilginx**, **custom phishlets**, **lure creation**, and **session hijacking**, your temporary infrastructure is no longer required — unless you plan to continue experimenting on your own.

In this section, you’ll learn how (and why) to safely **destroy your DigitalOcean droplet** to prevent **unnecessary charges** and reduce **attack surface exposure**.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/611-destroy-your-digital-ocean-droplet#user-content-how-to-destroy-your-droplet)How to Destroy Your Droplet

1. Log in to your DigitalOcean dashboard.
2. Navigate to **“Droplets”** from the left-hand menu.
3. Select your phishing VPS (the one running Evilginx2).
4. Click the **“Destroy”** tab at the top.
5. Click **“Destroy this Droplet”** and confirm.

This will immediately shut down and remove the VPS and its data.

> **Warning:** This action is irreversible. Be sure to back up anything you want to keep (e.g., session logs, phishlets) before proceeding.
