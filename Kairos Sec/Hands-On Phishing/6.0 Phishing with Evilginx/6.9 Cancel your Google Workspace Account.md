# Cancel Your Google Workspace Account

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/612-cancel-your-google-workspace-account#user-content-purpose)Purpose

During the hands-on phishing labs, you set up a **Google Workspace account** to simulate sending phishing emails from a realistic domain. That workspace account was likely created with Google’s **14-day free trial**.

Now that you've completed the infrastructure portion of the course, **you no longer need this account** — unless you plan to continue experimenting on your own.

To avoid being charged once the trial ends, you should **cancel your Google Workspace account** now.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/612-cancel-your-google-workspace-account#user-content-why-you-should-cancel)Why You Should Cancel

- **Avoid Automatic Billing:** After 14 days, Google will begin charging unless you manually cancel.
- **No Longer Needed:** The phishing infrastructure portion of the course is complete.
- **Security:** Leaving unused services active (with access to your custom domain) creates unnecessary exposure.

---

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/612-cancel-your-google-workspace-account#user-content-how-to-cancel-your-google-workspace-account)How to Cancel Your Google Workspace Account

1. Go to [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com/) and sign in as your Workspace administrator.
2. In the Admin Console, click **Billing** in the left-hand menu.
3. Click into your **Google Workspace subscription**.
4. Click **Cancel Subscription** and follow the prompts.

> **Important:** Canceling your Workspace account will not affect your domain registration with Namecheap. However, your domain's email delivery settings will revert, so consider deleting any related DNS records (e.g., MX, SPF, DKIM).

- A payment method is added
