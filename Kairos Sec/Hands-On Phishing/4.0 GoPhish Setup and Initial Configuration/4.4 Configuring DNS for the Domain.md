### **Overview**

This brief video provides an important **follow-up step** that ensures your phishing infrastructure is properly configured. Specifically, it focuses on updating DNS settings in **Namecheap** to point your custom domain to your **DigitalOcean Droplet**.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/44-configuring-dns-for-the-domain#user-content-step-by-step-instructions)**Step-by-Step Instructions**

**Log into Namecheap**:

- Go to [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/) and log in to your account.
- Navigate to the **“Domain List”**, locate your domain, and click **Manage**.

**Open Advanced DNS Settings**:

- After selecting your domain, go to the **“Advanced DNS”** tab to view and manage records.

**Add an A Record**:

- Click **Add New Record**.
- Choose **A Record** from the dropdown menu.
- In the **Host** field, enter `@` to represent the root domain.
- In the **Value** field, input the **IP address** of your **DigitalOcean Droplet**.

**Confirm the Configuration**:

- Once saved, the domain is now properly linked to your droplet, ensuring that traffic is directed correctly when launching phishing pages or payloads.

### [](https://www.hacksmarter.org/courses/3f9917b1-13b0-48f4-b43f-387e245656e7/take/44-configuring-dns-for-the-domain#user-content-why-this-matters)**Why This Matters**

- Associating your domain with your infrastructure is a critical part of building **trustworthy phishing templates**.
- Misconfigured DNS records can prevent your campaign from being reachable or trigger unnecessary troubleshooting delays.
- Ensuring this is set up now will make the **upcoming labs and campaign execution much smoother**.
