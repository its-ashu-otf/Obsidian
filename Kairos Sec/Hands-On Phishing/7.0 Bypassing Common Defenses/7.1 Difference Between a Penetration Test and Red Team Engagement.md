### **Difference Between a Penetration Test and Red Team Engagement**

A phishing email is useless if it never hits the inbox. This section explains why getting past spam filters is so important and sets the stage for techniques to do just that.

Before diving into those tactics, there’s a key distinction to understand: **penetration testing** vs. **red teaming**.

In most phishing-focused pen tests, the internal security team already knows what’s happening. They’ll often allow list your domain so emails go straight to users without filters getting in the way. The goal here isn’t stealth—it’s speed and coverage. You're working against the clock to find as many issues as possible and document them clearly.

Red teaming is a whole different game. You're simulating a real attacker, often with a specific objective—like moving money or accessing sensitive data—and the defenders aren’t in on it. That means you need to stay under the radar and actually **evade** email defenses. It’s not just about phishing users—it's about achieving goals without tipping anyone off.

Even if you’re mainly doing pen tests, understanding red team tactics gives you an edge. It can help in interviews, expand your skillset, and open doors to more advanced work down the road.
