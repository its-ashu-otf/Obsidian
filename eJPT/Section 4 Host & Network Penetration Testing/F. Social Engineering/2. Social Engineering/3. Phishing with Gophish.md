# 🛠️ GoPhish

**GoPhish** is a powerful **open-source phishing simulation framework** used by **penetration testers, red teamers, and security professionals** to **launch and manage phishing campaigns** within controlled environments.

---

### 🧰 **GoPhish Feature Set**

GoPhish provides a comprehensive suite of tools for planning, executing, and analyzing phishing simulations. Below are its core capabilities:

---

### 📦 1. **Campaign Creation**

- **Purpose:** Launch highly customized phishing simulations.
    
- **Key Capabilities:**
    
    - Create multiple concurrent campaigns.
        
    - Assign unique templates, landing pages, and target groups to each campaign.
        
    - Tailor each campaign to specific social engineering scenarios or organizational units.
        

---

### 📝 2. **Email Template Editor**

- **Built-In WYSIWYG Editor:**
    
    - Intuitive drag-and-drop interface.
        
    - Supports HTML and plain-text email formats.
        
- **Advanced Customization:**
    
    - Insert variables (e.g., first name, email) for personalization.
        
    - Embed phishing links or malicious payloads as needed for testing.
        

---

### 👥 3. **Target Management**

- **Segmentation Options:**
    
    - Group targets based on **role, department, location**, or any custom tags.
        
- **Import Support:**
    
    - CSV-based bulk import of recipients with custom fields.
        
- **Realistic Targeting:** Enables scenario-based testing mimicking advanced spear-phishing tactics.
    

---

### 🌐 4. **Landing Page Creation**

- **Customizable Pages:**
    
    - Clone real login pages or design unique phishing portals.
        
- **Data Capture:**
    
    - Capture submitted credentials, tokens, or custom form inputs.
        
- **Redirect Options:**
    
    - Redirect victims to a legitimate page after submission (for stealthier campaigns).
        

---

### 📊 5. **Tracking and Reporting**

- **Real-Time Metrics:**
    
    - Email delivered/opened
        
    - Link clicked
        
    - Data submitted
        
- **Detailed Reports:**
    
    - Exportable results in CSV/JSON
        
    - Campaign performance summaries
        
    - Per-user interaction logs
        

---

### ⏱️ 6. **Scheduling and Automation**

- **Campaign Scheduling:**
    
    - Launch campaigns on specific dates and times.
        
- **Recurring Tests:**
    
    - Automate periodic phishing assessments for long-term awareness tracking.
        

---

### 🛡️ **Security Awareness Use-Case**

These features make GoPhish ideal for:

- Continuous employee awareness training
    
- Realistic red team social engineering assessments
    
- Validating the effectiveness of technical controls (e.g., email filters)
    

---

### 🎯 **Common Use Cases**

- ✅ **Phishing Awareness Training**: Evaluate employee susceptibility and train them to recognize phishing attempts.
    
- ✅ **Red Team Operations**: Simulate real-world phishing as part of social engineering tests.
    
- ✅ **Security Posture Assessment**: Measure the effectiveness of current security awareness programs.
    

---

### 🚀 **Workflow in Brief**

1. **Create Email Template** → Craft a realistic phishing email.
    
2. **Design Landing Page** → Mimic a login portal to capture data.
    
3. **Set Up User Groups** → Define targets (import via CSV or manually).
    
4. **Launch Campaign** → Schedule and send phishing emails.
    
5. **Analyze Results** → Track interactions and generate reports.
    

---

### ⚠️ **Legal & Ethical Note**

> Only use GoPhish in environments **you have explicit permission** to test. Unauthorized phishing campaigns are illegal and unethical.
