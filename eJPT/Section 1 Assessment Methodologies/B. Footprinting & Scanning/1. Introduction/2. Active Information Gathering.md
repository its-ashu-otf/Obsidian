## 🔐 **Penetration Testing Methodology – Explained**

Pen testing follows a step-by-step approach to identify, exploit, and report security weaknesses. This methodology ensures tests are **organized, effective, and repeatable**.

---

### 1️⃣ **Information Gathering**

> **Goal:** Collect details about the target system/network.

#### 🔸 _Passive Information Gathering_

- **OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence):** Gather info from public sources (Google, Shodan, social media, domain records, etc.)
    
- No direct interaction with the target, so it's stealthy.
    

#### 🔸 _Active Information Gathering_

- Interacts with the target to extract info.
    
- Tools like **Nmap** are used for:
    
    - **Network Mapping:** Identify the network layout.
        
    - **Host Discovery:** Find active devices.
        
    - **Port Scanning:** Detect open ports.
        
    - **Service & OS Detection:** Identify running services and OS types.
        

---

### 2️⃣ **Enumeration**

> **Goal:** Dig deeper into discovered hosts/services.

#### 🔸 _Service & OS Enumeration_

- **Service Enumeration:** Learn what services (e.g., FTP, SMB, HTTP) are running.
    
- **User Enumeration:** Discover usernames on the system.
    
- **Share Enumeration:** Find shared folders/drives on the network.
    

💡 _Think of it as identifying “who’s there and what they’re using.”_

---

### 3️⃣ **Exploitation (Initial Access)**

> **Goal:** Gain access by exploiting vulnerabilities.

#### 🔸 _Vulnerability Analysis & Threat Modeling_

- Identify weaknesses in software/configurations.
    
- Tools like **Nessus**, **Nmap NSE**, or **SearchSploit** may help.
    

#### 🔸 _Exploitation_

- Use or develop exploits to take control.
    
- Focus on services with known CVEs or misconfigurations.
    

---

### 4️⃣ **Post-Exploitation**

> **Goal:** Expand access, gather more data, maintain persistence.

- **Local Enumeration:** Explore the compromised system.
    
- **Privilege Escalation:** Gain higher-level access (admin/root).
    
- **Credential Access:** Steal passwords/hashes/tokens.
    
- **Persistence:** Set up backdoors to regain access later.
    
- **Defense Evasion:** Avoid detection by antivirus or logging.
    
- **Lateral Movement:** Move across systems within the network.
    

---

### 5️⃣ **Reporting**

> **Goal:** Document findings and suggest improvements.

- **Report Writing:** Clearly explain what was found, how it was exploited, and its impact.
    
- **Recommendations:** Suggest fixes (e.g., patching, config changes, hardening).
    

---

### ✅ Quick Summary Notes:

```
1. Information Gathering – Passive (OSINT) and Active (Scanning, Mapping)
2. Enumeration – Find users, services, and shares.
3. Exploitation – Gain initial access via vulnerabilities.
4. Post-Exploitation – Expand control, maintain access.
5. Reporting – Document and recommend fixes.
```

## 🧭 **Active Information Gathering – Penetration Testing**

### 📌 **Definition:**

**Active information gathering**, in the context of penetration testing, refers to the phase of the assessment where the tester actively interacts with the target system or network to collect data and identify potential vulnerabilities.

Unlike passive recon (which is stealthy and non-intrusive), **active methods involve sending packets, scanning ports, or probing services**, which can trigger logs or alerts.

---

### ⚙️ **Key Techniques:**

- **Network Mapping:** Identify the network layout and topology.
    
- **Host Discovery:** Find live hosts using tools like `ping`, `Nmap`, or `arp-scan`.
    
- **Port Scanning:** Detect open/closed/filtered ports on a host.
    
- **Service Detection:** Identify services running on open ports (e.g., HTTP, FTP, SSH).
    
- **OS Fingerprinting:** Determine the target’s operating system.
    

---

### 🛠️ **Common Tools:**

- **Nmap**
    
- **Netcat**
    
- **Masscan**
    
- **Hping3**
    
- **Traceroute**
    

---

### ⚠️ **Things to Consider:**

- **Can be noisy** – leaves traces in logs and may alert defenders.
    
- Requires **proper authorization** in real-world engagements.
    
- Should follow ethical guidelines and scope boundaries.
    

---

### ✅ Quick Summary:

```
Active Info Gathering = Direct interaction with target.
Examples: Scanning, port probing, service detection.
Goal: Identify vulnerabilities and gather actionable data.
Tool of choice: Nmap.
```
