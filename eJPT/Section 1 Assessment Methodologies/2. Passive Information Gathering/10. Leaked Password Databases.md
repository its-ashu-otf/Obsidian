Leaked password databases are collections of credentials (usernames, emails, and passwords) that have been exposed due to data breaches. These databases often end up on the dark web or hacker forums, where cybercriminals use them for credential stuffing, phishing, and other attacks.

### **How Leaked Password Databases Are Used:**

1. **Credential Stuffing** – Attackers try leaked passwords on multiple accounts, exploiting users who reuse passwords.
    
2. **Phishing & Social Engineering** – Attackers use leaked credentials to craft convincing phishing attacks.
    
3. **Brute Force Attacks** – Cybercriminals analyze password patterns to improve brute-force techniques.
    
4. **Selling on Dark Web** – Databases are sold or traded on underground forums.
    

### **How to Check if Your Password is Leaked:**

- **Have I Been Pwned (HIBP)** – [https://haveibeenpwned.com/](https://haveibeenpwned.com/) allows users to check if their email or password was exposed.
    
- **Dehashed** – [https://www.dehashed.com/](https://www.dehashed.com/) provides advanced searches for compromised credentials.
    

### **How to Protect Yourself:**

✅ Use **unique passwords** for each account.  
✅ Enable **multi-factor authentication (MFA)**.  
✅ Use a **password manager** to generate and store strong passwords.  
✅ Regularly check for breaches and update passwords if necessary.
